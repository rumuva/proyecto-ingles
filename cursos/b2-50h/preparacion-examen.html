B2 First · Reading and Use of English

Formato oficial. 75 minutos, 7 partes y 52 preguntas. Las partes 1–4 trabajan el uso léxico y gramatical. Las partes 5–7 trabajan comprensión detallada, cohesión y búsqueda de información. En práctica, dedica 27 minutos al primer bloque, 40 al segundo y 8 a revisar. Ajusta este reparto tras probar la muestra oficial.

Parte 1 · Opción múltiple. Lee primero todo el texto, subraya las palabras pegadas al hueco y prueba la opción en la frase completa. Distingue colocación de sinonimia.

The organisers wanted to (1) ___ attention to the problems faced by evening workers. They (2) ___ a short survey before (3) ___ any decisions. The results (4) ___ doubts about the original timetable.

    1. draw · B) turn · C) lift · D) lead
    1. produced · B) carried out · C) brought up · D) put off
    1. setting · B) doing · C) drawing · D) making
    1. grew · B) lifted · C) raised · D) increased

Parte 2 · Open cloze. Escribe exactamente una palabra por hueco. En este texto, identifica si el hueco afecta a comparación, relativo, contraste o duración.

The revised plan is not as expensive (1) ___ the original. The room (2) ___ we met has been refurbished. (3) ___ the lift is working, the side door still needs to be unlocked. The team has been checking access (4) ___ Monday.

Parte 3 · Word formation. Identifica la categoría que exige la frase. Comprueba si hace falta un prefijo, un plural o un cambio interno.

  1. The ___ of evening support forced us to redesign the plan. (AVAILABLE)
  2. Although the figures look impressive, the headline is ___. (LEAD)
  3. The decision was ___ justified in the report. (CLEAR)
  4. The new entrance is fully ___ to wheelchair users. (ACCESS)
  5. Two ___ were found between the invoice and the records. (DISCREPANT)

Parte 4 · Key word transformations. Completa la segunda frase con dos a cinco palabras, incluida la palabra clave tal como aparece. Comprueba si las condiciones, el tiempo y el punto de vista siguen siendo los mismos.

  1. The tutor started working here three years ago. BEEN · The tutor ___ here for three years.
  2. They did not inform us about the delay. TOLD · We ___ about the delay.
  3. The extra support is only available if you book before Friday. UNLESS · You cannot get the extra support ___ before Friday.
  4. The first venue is cheaper than the second. AS · The second venue is ___ the first.
  5. “Why don’t we try a pilot?” said Mina. SUGGESTED · Mina ___ a pilot.
  6. The team should have checked the figures before publishing. OUGHT · The team ___ the figures before publishing.

Partes 5 a 7 · Lectura detallada y cohesión

Parte 5 · Opción múltiple. Lee el primer párrafo y responde por qué la autora separa capacity de reach.

The sessions filled within a day, which the centre took as a sign of success. Yet the waiting list had been disabled, so the centre could only count those who obtained a place. To me, this was evidence of the programme’s capacity, not of its reach. A larger room might let more people in, but a different starting time might reach a different group altogether.

  1. Porque una sala grande siempre mejora el programa.
  2. Porque llenar plazas demuestra ocupación, pero no a quién se está llegando.
  3. Porque el coste depende del horario.
  4. Porque la sala estaba vacía.

Parte 6 · Texto con hueco. Identifica el referente de that risk y comprueba que la frase elegida enlaza también con la oración posterior.

The new timetable made an evening session possible. However, it took for granted that everyone could travel home after eight. [___] To reduce that risk, the organisers arranged a direct bus to the station.

  1. Some participants might otherwise have been left without a safe way home.
  2. The printing costs were below budget.
  3. Participants received their certificates a week later.

Parte 7 · Emparejamiento. ¿Quién menciona un criterio de cancelación? ¿Quién pide medir a quienes quedan fuera? ¿Quién pone una condición a su apoyo? Puedes usar una opción una vez o más.

A. Ravi: “I’d support the extra evening provided there is an accessible route.”
B. Jo: “Let’s ask people who did not secure a place why the original booking failed.”
C. Elena: “If attendance remains below half the available places for two weeks, pause the trial.”

B2 First · Writing

Formato oficial. Dos textos de 140–190 palabras en 80 minutos. Primero el ensayo obligatorio con dos notas dadas y una tercera idea propia. En la parte 2 se elige una tarea, que puede pedir artículo, correo o carta, reseña o informe en B2 First. Para cada texto practica 5 minutos de planificación, 30 de redacción y 5 de revisión.

Ensayo obligatorio

Some people believe community courses should always be offered online. Do you agree? Write about access, quality of feedback and your own idea.

Subraya always. Formula una postura que responda al alcance de esa palabra. Dedica un párrafo a los beneficios de acceso, otro al tipo de retroalimentación y otro a tu idea nueva. Usa una concesión que apoye tu criterio final.

Parte 2 · Elige una tarea

  • Report. You were asked to review a two-week language workshop. Write a report for the manager outlining which learners attended, what barriers remained and two recommendations based on your findings.
  • Review. Write a review of a language-learning resource for working adults. Evaluate two features, explain what could improve and say who would benefit.
  • Email. A centre wants your group to move to a new venue. Write to the organiser explaining two access requirements, asking for written confirmation and suggesting a workable alternative.
  • Variante de práctica de artículo. Write an article for a local website explaining how volunteers can help newcomers feel welcome. Use an example and give one practical piece of advice.

Corrección. Valora 0–5 por criterio en la escala interna del curso: content (se cumplen las notas), communicative achievement (género, público y efecto), organisation (desarrollo y cohesión) y language (repertorio y control). Justifica cada puntuación con una frase del texto y reescribe el párrafo con menor puntuación.

B2 First · Listening

Formato oficial. Aproximadamente 40 minutos, 4 partes y 30 preguntas. Se escuchan las grabaciones dos veces. Usa la primera escucha para detectar contexto, hablante y propósito. En la segunda, comprueba el detalle y anota dónde se descarta el distractor. Estas microtareas entrenan los cuatro formatos con fragmentos más cortos que un examen completo.

  1. Opción múltiple corta. Escucha el diálogo de la unidad 4. ¿Qué impide confirmar el paquete? A) El precio se sale del presupuesto. B) El soporte del viernes no está confirmado. C) El primer mes ya está pagado.
  2. Completar frases. Escucha la pista del diagnóstico final. Completa en inglés con datos del audio: «The pilot would last ___ weeks» y «The venue must confirm access by Tuesday at ___».
  3. Emparejar hablantes. Escucha tres testimonios independientes. Asocia A, B y C con las razones 1) horario de trabajo, 2) viaje de vuelta y 3) falta de equipo. Sobran 4) falta de plazas y 5) dificultad del contenido.
  4. Entrevista larga. Escucha el diálogo de la unidad 6. ¿Qué postura expresa la entrevistada? A) Las cifras de reserva son incorrectas. B) El taller fracasó. C) Las cifras muestran demanda en el horario ofertado y la generalización es excesiva. Justifica la respuesta en una frase.

B2 First · Speaking

Formato oficial. 4 partes y unos 14 minutos por pareja. En la preparación, practica entrevista, turno individual con dos fotos, decisión colaborativa y discusión relacionada. Usa una persona que cronometre.

  1. Entrevista. “What kind of learning environment suits you best?” Da una respuesta de tres frases con preferencia, motivo y ejemplo.
  2. Turno largo. Usa las dos fotografías del portal como estímulo. “Compare the pictures and say what challenges each group of travellers might face.” Habla un minuto, relaciona las fotos y explica un reto en cada caso. La otra persona responde unos 30 segundos a “Which situation would be easier for visitors who don’t know the area?”
  3. Discusión colaborativa. “What should a city improve to make travel easier?” Comentad clearer signs, more frequent services, step-free access, ticket prices y clearer updates. Contrastad razones durante unos dos minutos y dedicad otro minuto a acordar una prioridad.
  4. Discusión. “To what extent can public transport change people’s choices?” Reacciona a un argumento concreto y distingue lo que sabes de lo que supones.

Evalúa gramática y vocabulario, desarrollo de ideas, pronunciación comprensible e interacción. Usa el vídeo oficial de Cambridge para calibrar duración y tipo de preguntas.

Escuela Oficial de Idiomas · Intermedio B2

La EOI de Andalucía evalúa comprensión oral y escrita, producción y coproducción oral y escrita y mediación. La mediación oral y escrita puede integrarse con sus correspondientes bloques de producción y coproducción. Consulta la guía de la convocatoria de tu escuela para confirmar extensión, tiempos y estructura. Las consignas siguientes son prácticas propias.

Comprensión y producción escrita

  1. Lee el caso de la unidad 10. Marca hechos confirmados, restricciones y datos pendientes. ¿Qué hace inviable Green? ¿Cuánto cuestan dos sesiones? ¿Qué equipo aún debes confirmar? ¿Cuándo hay que informar al grupo?
  2. Escribe a la organización como responsable de accesibilidad. Pide confirmación de la hora y del equipo. Explica por qué una sola sesión en Blue no sirve. Propón mantener dos plazas de acceso prioritario hasta confirmar la asignación. Usa 140–170 palabras en esta práctica.

Comprensión y coproducción oral

  1. Escucha la unidad 3. Diferencia qué registra el escaneo, qué hipótesis siguen abiertas y cuándo se solicitará el registro.
  2. En pareja, negociad la respuesta a una persona que exige saber quién se equivocó. Una persona defiende la transparencia, la otra evita atribuir responsabilidades sin evidencia. Explicad qué podéis confirmar hoy y cuándo se enviará la siguiente actualización.

Mediación escrita

Fuente. Un centro ofrece dos sedes para una sesión. Sala Norte 18 plazas, acceso sin escalones, coste £90, sin proyector. Sala Sur 30 plazas, acceso por escaleras, £105 con proyector. Norte está libre a las 10:00 y a las 12:00. Dos turnos cuestan £140 si se confirma antes del miércoles a las 13:00. El proyector portátil está solicitado y queda por confirmar. La cafetería abre a las 9:00.

Consigna. Escribe un mensaje de 100–130 palabras en inglés a una coordinadora que espera 24 personas, necesita acceso sin escalones y dispone de £150. Adapta requisitos, precio, plazo y equipo. Evita incluir detalles que no cambien su decisión.

Mediación oral

La coordinadora de la tarea anterior solo puede asistir antes de las 11:00. Explica en inglés qué opciones tiene durante 60–90 segundos. Verifica que distingue solicitud, reserva y confirmación. Si pregunta si el proyector está incluido, señala qué dato falta y quién puede aclararlo.

Rúbrica formativa. Da un punto por identificar destinatario y necesidad, seleccionar los datos relevantes, mantener cifras y condiciones, reformular con claridad y dar un próximo paso útil. En la mediación, penaliza una garantía inventada aunque la gramática sea correcta.

Plan para comprobar si estás preparado

  1. En la unidad 1, identifica objetivo y convocatoria. Haz una primera tarea de lectura y otra de expresión oral con tiempo.
  2. En las unidades 2–8, alterna un formato receptivo y otro productivo. Repite el tipo de tarea que te cuesta con el modelo cerrado.
  3. En la unidad 9, practica mediación oral y escrita si vas a la EOI. Para Cambridge, repite un ensayo y otro género con el tiempo total de Writing.
  4. Termina con una muestra oficial completa y registra la nota de cada destreza, el tiempo empleado y el patrón de error. La práctica interna sirve para indicar qué repasar. Compara después con las escalas de la prueba elegida.

Claves de B2 First

Reading and Use of English. Parte 1: 1 A (draw attention to), 2 B (carry out a survey), 3 D (make a decision), 4 C (raise doubts). Parte 2: 1 as, 2 where (también in which sería gramatical, pero excede el límite de una palabra), 3 Although o While, 4 since. Parte 3: 1 unavailability, 2 misleading, 3 clearly, 4 accessible, 5 discrepancies. Parte 4: 1 has been working, 2 were not told, 3 unless you book, 4 not as cheap as, 5 suggested trying, 6 ought to have checked. Parte 5: B. Parte 6: A. Parte 7: criterio C, demanda B, condición A.

Listening. 1 B. 2 four, noon. 3 A–1, B–2, C–3. 4 C. La entrevistada reconoce que las plazas se llenaron y acota lo que ese hecho demuestra.

Speaking · modelo de turno largo. Both photographs show places where people can begin a journey in London. At the Underground entrance, visitors might find the network convenient but may need help choosing the right line or finding step-free access. In the street with the bus, the route is more visible and passengers can see familiar landmarks. However, they may not know which stop to use, and traffic could make the journey less predictable. I’d give first-time visitors clear directions to the correct platform or stop rather than assuming that a map is enough. Para la discusión, cada participante debe retomar al menos una idea ajena y cerrar con un acuerdo verificable.

Modelo de ensayo

Community courses should be offered online when doing so improves access, but they should not be restricted to that format. An online course can help people who work irregular hours or live far from a centre. However, anyone without a reliable connection or a suitable device may find it harder to take part.

Face-to-face teaching also has advantages when learners need immediate feedback. For example, a tutor can see how someone is using a tool and correct a mistake before it becomes a habit. Nevertheless, a well-designed online course can support feedback through recordings and short individual sessions.

Another issue is the cost of running both formats. A centre could start with one in-person session and move some practice online rather than providing two complete courses. On balance, I would choose the format according to learners’ access requirements and the skills they need to practise.

Modelo de informe

Attendance and access

Most weekday places were taken by people who already used the centre. Several shift workers said they could not attend at the time offered. Although all places were booked, the system did not record unsuccessful applications, so the number of people affected remains unknown.

Recommendations

First, I recommend offering one evening session during a four-week pilot. That would allow us to see whether the timetable was a barrier, although we should confirm step-free access before opening bookings. Secondly, the centre should introduce a short waiting list. It could ask applicants to select a preferred time and indicate whether they need any help travelling to the venue.

Review

The team should compare attendance, unanswered requests and staffing costs before deciding whether to continue. This would give management a broader view of the programme’s reach than booking figures alone.

Otros géneros. En la reseña, incorpora dos observaciones concretas, un inconveniente y una recomendación para el público. En el correo, usa asunto informativo, dos necesidades explícitas y una alternativa condicionada. En el artículo, abre con una pregunta al lector, incorpora un ejemplo y termina con un consejo práctico. El informe agrupa hallazgos, recomendaciones y revisión como se muestra arriba.

Claves y modelos de la EOI

Lectura: Green queda sin ascensor. Dos sesiones cuestan £140. Falta confirmar equipo y hora del segundo turno. Hay que escribir al grupo hoy. Escucha: el registro acredita el escaneo de las etiquetas. Siguen abiertas la duplicación y el intercambio tras el escaneo. Se acordó solicitar el registro para mañana.

Modelo de coproducción escrita. Dear team, thank you for sending the revised venue options. One Room Blue session cannot take everyone because it holds twenty people and we have twenty-six participants. Two sessions would meet our access and capacity requirements. Before confirming, could you send the second start time and say whether the quoted £140 includes a projector? Please also confirm that the route from the entrance to Room Blue is step-free. I suggest holding two accessible places in the first group until we have checked the individual arrangements. If the equipment is not included, please give us the total price so we can compare it with our £150 limit. I can send a provisional message to all participants today and follow it up once the booking is confirmed. Could you also tell me who will meet participants at reception and direct them to the correct session? Kind regards, Alex.

Modelo de mediación escrita. Hi, Room North is the suitable option for step-free access. One session only takes 18 people, so we’d need to split our group of 24. Two sessions, at ten and noon, would cost £140 if we confirm before Wednesday at one. Room South has enough space for everyone and includes a projector, but it is only accessible by stairs. I’d ask the venue to hold both slots in Room North while they confirm whether the portable projector is available. Our budget leaves £10, so we also need to check whether there is a separate charge before booking. Please tell me who needs to attend the first session.

Modelo de mediación oral. You can attend the ten o’clock session if we go ahead with Room North. It is step-free, and two sessions would accommodate the whole group at a cost of £140. We haven’t confirmed the booking or the portable projector yet. I’ll ask whether there’s an extra charge and hold a morning place for you if the room is confirmed. Could you tell me whether arriving a few minutes before ten would work for you?

Modelos adicionales de escritura B2 First

Reseña. An English course that fits around work. I tried an online speaking course for adults who work shifts. Its strongest feature was the short recorded lesson at the start of each week. I could watch it twice before practising, which made difficult instructions easier to follow. The second useful feature was the partner task. Each learner had different information, so we had to listen and ask questions instead of reading from a script. However, partners were assigned at a fixed time, and on two occasions I could not attend because my shift changed. It would be better to offer two possible meeting times or allow participants to arrange one themselves. The written feedback was useful once it arrived, but waiting a week made it difficult to remember what I had said. I would recommend the course to people who already understand everyday English and want more speaking practice, especially if they can protect one evening a week.

Correo. Dear Sam, thank you for suggesting the new venue for next month’s training day. Before we confirm the change, could you check two access requirements? First, one participant needs a step-free route from the street to the teaching room. The entrance may be accessible, but we also need to know whether the lift is currently working. Secondly, several participants will arrive by bus, so please send the nearest stop and clear directions from there. Could you confirm both points in writing by Wednesday afternoon? If the lift cannot be guaranteed, we could hold the session in the ground-floor room at the original centre. It is smaller, but we could divide the group into two short sessions if the trainer is available. Please also let me know whether that option would change the cost. Once we have the details, I’ll contact the participants with one clear set of instructions. Kind regards, Alex.

Artículo. How to make a newcomer feel welcome. Have you ever arrived somewhere new and been unsure whom to ask for help? A small gesture can make that first day much easier. Volunteers can start by introducing themselves and showing people where to find practical things, such as the nearest bus stop or the reception desk. However, giving someone a long list of information all at once may be overwhelming. At our local centre, a volunteer offered to walk with a newcomer to the library rather than simply handing them a map. They chatted on the way, and the newcomer felt able to ask questions that had seemed too small to mention before. Another useful step is to check what the person needs instead of making assumptions. They may want help booking an activity, or they may just need a quiet place to wait. Ask one clear question, listen to the answer and agree on something you can actually do.

Coproducción oral para la EOI. A: I understand the customer wants to know who made the mistake. We can confirm that three labels showed R7, but the records do not establish how that happened. B: Wouldn’t it be more transparent to tell them about the duplicate print job? A: Yes, as long as we explain that it is only one possible explanation. Let’s tell them what we found and promise a factual update by noon tomorrow. B: Agreed. I’ll request the transport record today and review the wording before we send it.

Transcripción de la pista adicional

  1. I’d arranged childcare and I had a computer I could borrow, but my manager moved my shift at short notice. The evening session was already my best option, and once I was working until eight I couldn’t make it.
  2. I could have left work early enough to join. What put me off was finding out that the last bus left just before the end. I didn’t want to have to leave halfway through every class.
  3. The time and travel suited me. I still didn’t sign up because my old phone couldn’t run the software we were meant to use. The centre provided a list of free resources, but as far as I could tell it didn’t lend devices.
